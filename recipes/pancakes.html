Pancakes

Description

These are soft, fluffy classic pancakes made from scratch with pantry staples — flour, eggs, milk, and a touch of vanilla. They come together in under 20 minutes and cook up golden on the outside with a light, airy inside. Perfect for a lazy weekend breakfast, topped with butter and maple syrup, fresh fruit, or just a dusting of icing sugar.

Ingredients:

  • 250 gram cake flower
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 0.5 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 350 millilitres milk
  • 45 grams melted butter, plus extra for cooking
  • 1 teaspoon vinilla extract
